About Hemamco

A Dammam-based contractor with the resources of the Kingdom’s best.

Founded in 2006, Hemamco Company Limited has grown through determination, skill and strong working ethics into a dynamic organisation of highly skilled, motivated professionals.

Today our resources are on par with any reputable construction company in the Kingdom — enabling us to execute everything from small works to multi-million riyal projects for national energy, utilities and petrochemical leaders.

  • Saudi Aramco & SABIC approved work standards
  • 10,000 m² fabrication facility in Dammam
  • Dedicated QA/QC and HSE departments
  • In-house fleet of 178+ equipment units
