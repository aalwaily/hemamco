Our Services

Leading engineering & construction services — on schedule, within budget and to the highest quality.

Leading engineering & construction

From small works to multi-million riyal projects

Since 2006 Hemamco has executed projects of every scale. On schedule, within budget, quality workmanship, teamwork and client satisfaction have always been our primary business ethics.

01

Civil & Earth Works

Civil engineering and earthworks with special expertise in site development, roads, asphalting and substation buildings.

Our team-oriented philosophy, value engineering, solid QA/QC and overall professionalism are recognised as a benchmark of industry standard.

  • Site development & grading
  • Roads & asphalt paving
  • Drainage & sewerage
  • Perimeter fencing & gates
02

Mechanical & Piping

Within sectors such as oil and gas, mining and metals, chemical processing and water, we provide a complete construction service — engineering, procurement, fabrication and field erection for mechanical, piping and electrical installation works through to commissioning.

  • Pipeline construction
  • Plant shutdowns
  • Tank maintenance
  • Commissioning support
03

Fabrication

Our Dammam fabrication facility is supervised by highly skilled mechanical engineers. Expertise spans oil & gas pipe spooling, process pipework, modular, skid-mounted and containerised fabrication, plate work for tanks, vessels and ducting, and heavy structural steel for mining, crane beams and platforms.

The fully equipped, covered 10,000 m² workshop includes CNC milling machines, lathes, large radial drills, coil cutting, mechanical saws, steel cutting and bending machines and a full range of welding equipment.

  • Pipe spooling
  • Skids & modules
  • Tanks & vessels
  • Heavy structural steel
04

Electrical Works

A wide range of electrical engineering tasks including overhead and underground power lines, fibre optic installation, switchgear and controls in substations, commercial and residential building wiring and street lighting — plus material, labour and construction cost estimation for budget preparation.

  • Overhead & underground lines
  • Substation switchgear
  • Fibre optics
  • Street lighting
05

Steel Erection

Steel erection for heavy-duty high-rise structures, metal buildings and signage. Steel erection is often the skeletal core of bridges, office buildings and commercial, retail and industrial structures.

  • Pipe racks & platforms
  • Metal buildings
  • Industrial structures
  • Telecom & power towers
