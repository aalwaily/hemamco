Safety Policy

Every employee is entitled to a safe and healthy work environment.

Loss Prevention Program

Safety is a condition of employment at Hemamco.

The Hemamco Loss Prevention Program is based on the premise that every employee is entitled to a safe and healthy work environment. It is designed specifically for the protection of our employees and visitors, and management and all employees are directed to make safety and loss prevention a top priority.

The goals we set are achieved through a cooperative effort among all employees and management. Safe work habits and awareness of all safety rules and policies are a condition of employment. All employees are required to attend training sessions to become familiar with these rules and to abide by them; they are enforced like any other company policy, and failure to comply can result in reprimand, suspension or termination.

All employees are encouraged to make suggestions that help maintain safe work conditions and to bring them to their supervisor’s attention. It is through our joint participation that accidents can be prevented — but only you can make safe work practices a habit.

Goals

  1. Annually reduce employee accidents and injuries until they stand at or near zero.
  2. Help develop safe work habits and attitudes among employees.
  3. Provide a channel of communication between employees and management.

Objectives

Establish a Loss Prevention Program that reduces injuries and accidents to a minimum — not merely keeping with, but surpassing the best experience of similar operations.

Tactics

  1. Review past injuries quarterly for trends and causes.
  2. Review the program annually to meet current safety needs.
  3. Provide supervisors and employees with safety training.
